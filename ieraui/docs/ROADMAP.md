# What I'd like to improve next

The first versions came from notes taken while using Forge. These remain the most useful next steps:

1. **Explain costs.** Show the total, what is payable now, and where a tax or restriction came from. “5 plus green” should be easy to read as six mana, and Lodestone Golem's extra cost should be named.
2. **Extend potential mana and X calculations.** Local 0.1.18 offers an auto-pay maximum after Other, accounting for colored costs, repeated X, taxes, reductions, and spending restrictions. A broader search covering mana filters, activation costs, and mutually exclusive combinations remains future work.
3. **Show combat consequences while assignments change.** Keep expected life damage, poison and lifelink separate, with assumptions visible.
4. **Make changes easier to notice.** Larger floating life, mana, poison and damage feedback, with a clear connection to the card or event that caused it.
5. **Explain unavailable and optional actions.** Equipment-granted abilities, shroud versus hexproof, optional blinks, and empty retrieval choices all need clearer feedback.
6. **Keep help out of the way.** A bounded, deliberately opened explanation area should replace long popups near gameplay controls.

These are plans, not features promised by the 0.1.12 download. Work should stay small enough to review and useful enough that it could be offered upstream independently.
